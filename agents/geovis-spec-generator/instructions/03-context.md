## What it is

Cozinhas Solidárias is a public application that maps community kitchens
fighting hunger in Brazil: where they are, how many people they serve, and how
that presence relates to social vulnerability, food insecurity and family
farming in each municipality. The target audience is anyone interested in food
security (journalists, public managers, researchers, the kitchen network
itself), not only GIS specialists. Every displayed datum is aggregated by
municipality. The individual kitchen registry contains personal data (address,
CNPJ, e-mail) and is never exposed row by row.
